Global Privacy Statement & Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Reference Model: Global Enterprise Compliance (DPDPA 2023 / GDPR / CCPA)

Your Choices at a Glance: You can manage or withdraw your cookie and tracker choices at any time using our interactive Preference Centre.

1. Introduction & Scope

G3 Shop ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Statement describes how we collect, process, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, submit inquiries through our contact forms, purchase products, or interact with our digital services.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal information depending on your interaction with our store:

  • Contact and Inquiry Data: Your name, email address, and message details when submitting inquiries through our Contact form or newsletter signup.
  • Transaction and Order Data: Items selected, cart details, shipping address, and order reference IDs to process and deliver purchases.
  • Device and Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and page browsing interactions gathered via tags and cookies, strictly subject to your consent.

3. Use of Cookies and Similar Tracking Technologies

Like enterprise organizations worldwide, our website uses cookies, web beacons, and pixels to deliver essential functionality, measure performance, and (with your affirmative consent) deliver relevant marketing.

We classify our cookies and trackers into four clear categories:

A. Strictly Necessary (Always Active)

These technologies are essential for the website to function securely and reliably. They maintain your shopping cart, protect against CSRF attacks, and remember your privacy choices. Under privacy laws, these cannot be switched off and do not require prior consent.

B. Functional Technologies

These enable enhanced website features and personalization, such as remembering your selected currency, language preferences, or tag management infrastructure (Google Tag Manager).

C. Analytics and Performance

These cookies allow us to count visits, analyze traffic sources, and understand how visitors move across pages, enabling us to measure and improve storefront performance. They remain blocked until you grant permission.

D. Advertising and Marketing Technologies

These trackers (including the Meta Pixel) are used by us and our advertising partners to measure campaign effectiveness and show you relevant announcements on other platforms. They are strictly blocked until you explicitly grant consent.

5. How to Manage and Withdraw Your Consent

You have full control over non-essential cookies. You can modify your choices or withdraw consent at any time through any of the following methods:

  1. Interactive Cookie Settings: Click the Cookie Settings link in our website footer or click the button below to reopen the Preference Centre:
  2. Browser Settings: You can block or delete cookies through your web browser's privacy settings. Note that disabling necessary cookies may break shopping cart functionality.
  3. Global Privacy Control (GPC): We automatically detect and honor browser Global Privacy Control signals, switching off advertising trackers when GPC is enabled.

6. Lawful Grounds for Processing

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and GDPR Art. 6, we process personal data under these lawful bases:

  • Consent (DPDPA s.6(1) / GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): For non-essential cookies (Analytics, Marketing) and marketing communications. Every consent decision is recorded in an immutable, tamper-evident ledger with Merkle tree inclusion proofs.
  • Contract Performance: To process orders, deliver purchases, and fulfill customer requests.
  • Legal Obligation & Legitimate Use: To comply with legal requirements, prevent fraud, and maintain platform security.

7. International Data Transfers

When you submit forms or interact with cloud-hosted services, personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your residence (including the United States and the European Economic Area). Such transfers are conducted under recognized lawful mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and regulatory cross-border transfer safeguards.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Information: Request a summary of personal data held about you and processing activities carried out.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request updating of inaccurate information or deletion of personal data no longer necessary.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Submit a complaint regarding personal data processing to our Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise privacy rights in case of death or incapacity (DPDPA s.14).

9. Data Protection & Grievance Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Statement, wish to exercise your data rights, or submit a grievance, please contact:

Data Protection & Grievance Officer: Privacy Office

Email: privacy@g3cyberspace.com

Response Timeline: We respond to all verified rights requests and grievances within the statutory period prescribed by law (within 30 days under GDPR / within the published grievance window under DPDPA).
